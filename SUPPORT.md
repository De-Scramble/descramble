# Getting Support

There are two routes: the free community route, and paid commercial support. Both are real, and the
free one is not a lesser version of the product.

## Free — community support

The core is free forever and so is community help. Use, in order of preference:

1. **Read the docs.** The README covers installation, the quickstart, configuration, and how the
   matching actually works.
2. **Search existing issues** — your question may already be answered.
3. **Open a Discussion** for questions, ideas, and "how do I…" — this is the right place for
   anything that is not a defect.
4. **Open an Issue** for reproducible bugs and concrete feature requests, using the templates.

Please do not use the security address for support questions; keep it clear for
vulnerability reports (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

**What free support means honestly:** it is best-effort, from maintainers doing this alongside
other work. Questions are usually answered, but there is no response-time guarantee, and there
cannot be one without someone paying for it. A clear, reproducible report gets a faster answer than
a vague one.

## Paid — commercial support from Deluge Limited

**Deluge Limited**, the project's steward, offers optional paid services for teams that need
guarantees rather than best-effort:

- **Integration** — connecting De-Scramble to your own sources, catalogue, and warehouse.
- **Hosting and operation** — running the pipeline for you.
- **Support with an SLA** — contracted response times, prioritised fixes, upgrade assistance.
- **Tuning** — comparison and blocking configuration adapted to your data and your match quality
  requirements.

Details: **https://descramble.dev/services** · to talk to someone: **support@descramble.dev**

None of this is required to use De-Scramble. Every capability in the core is available to everyone
under the Apache-2.0 licence, and running it yourself is a fully supported, first-class choice —
see the open-core promise in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). Paid services buy convenience,
guarantees, and our time. They do not unlock features.

## Sponsorship

There is deliberately **no `FUNDING.yml` and no sponsor button** in this repository. The project
does not solicit donations: it is funded by Deluge Limited's services work, and adding a donation
prompt to a commercially-stewarded project would muddy that. If you want to support the work, use
the project and tell us what broke.
